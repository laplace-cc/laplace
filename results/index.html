Late Start Success

Started in October. Admitted to Brown ED.

Profile

Senior, 3.95 UW GPA, 1520 SAT. Strong extracurriculars but no clear narrative tying them together. Came to us 3 weeks before the Early Decision deadline.

What we did

Rebuilt her personal statement from scratch around a through-line we identified in her activities. Rewrote 4 supplemental essays. Repositioned her activity list to emphasize depth over breadth.

STEM Spike

Research-focused junior. Admitted to MIT and Stanford.

Profile

Junior, 4.0 UW GPA, 1580 SAT, AIME qualifier. Two published research papers but struggled to explain his work in a way admissions officers could connect with.

What we did

Worked on translating technical research into accessible, compelling narratives. Built a school list balancing STEM powerhouses with liberal arts options. Coached him through MIT and Stanford interviews.

8th Grade Start

Started in 8th grade. Full ride at a top-20 school.

Profile

Started working with us before high school even began. No standout activities yet, but strong academic foundation and wide interests.

What we did

Built a 4-year roadmap from scratch. Guided course selection, identified competition opportunities (Conrad Challenge, Diamond Challenge), matched with a research mentor, and developed a leadership trajectory. By junior year, had multiple national recognitions.

Result

Admitted to Duke with a full merit scholarship. Also accepted at Vanderbilt, UVA, and WashU.

International Student

International applicant. Admitted to Harvard.

International student, top of class, perfect scores. Strong profile on paper but sounded like every other high-achieving international applicant.

What we did

Admitted to Harvard (RD). Also accepted at Yale, Princeton, and Duke.

Diamond Challenge Winner
