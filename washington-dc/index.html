Why DC-Area Students Need a Strategy

The DC metro area — including Northern Virginia and suburban Maryland — is one of the most educated and achievement-oriented regions in the country. Students from Thomas Jefferson High School for Science and Technology, Georgetown Prep, Sidwell Friends, and top public schools across the area are competing at the national level for spots at the same selective universities.

DC-area students often have strong public service and policy narratives, but those narratives are common in a region defined by proximity to government. Standing out means finding what's genuinely distinctive about your story — and presenting it compellingly. That's the work Laplace does with every student.
