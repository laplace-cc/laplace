<p>"How much does this cost?" is the first question nearly every family asks us, and the honest answer is that college consulting prices vary more than almost any other service in education. The same essay help can cost a few hundred dollars or several thousand depending on who you hire. Here's how pricing actually works across the industry.</p>

<h2>The common pricing models</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Hourly.</strong> Independent counselors and tutors often charge by the hour. Good for targeted help, such as reviewing a list or a single essay.</li>
<li><strong>Package by service.</strong> A fixed fee for a defined scope, like an essay package covering the personal statement and supplements, or a roadmap for a younger student.</li>
<li><strong>Comprehensive multi-year.</strong> Ongoing support from early high school through applications. These are the most expensive, and at large national firms they can reach five figures and well beyond.</li>
</ul>

<h2>What drives the price</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Scope and duration.</strong> Senior-year essay help is a very different engagement from a four-year plan starting in 9th grade.</li>
<li><strong>Who does the work.</strong> At some firms, the person who sells the package isn't the person who works with your student. Ask.</li>
<li><strong>Brand and overhead.</strong> Big firms spend heavily on marketing and offices. That cost is in the price.</li>
<li><strong>Live hours vs. offline review.</strong> Unlimited written feedback and a fixed number of live sessions are priced very differently.</li>
</ul>

<h2>How to tell if a price is fair</h2>
<p>A fair price is one where you know exactly what you're getting. Before you sign anything, make sure the agreement answers:</p>
<ol>
<li>Which specific deliverables are included (essays, list, activities list, roadmap, interview prep)?</li>
<li>How many live hours, and is offline feedback unlimited?</li>
<li>Who is the consultant, and what is their own track record?</li>
<li>How quickly do they respond to emails and drafts?</li>
<li>What happens if a deliverable isn't met?</li>
</ol>
<p>Be wary of anyone who guarantees admission to a specific school. No ethical consultant can, and no consultant should ever write a student's essays for them.</p>

<h2>How Laplace prices</h2>
<p>We don't publish a one-size price list, because a senior who needs essay help in October and a 9th grader building a four-year plan need completely different things. Instead, we scope each engagement individually and send a specific price within 24 hours of a free call, with every deliverable written into the agreement.
