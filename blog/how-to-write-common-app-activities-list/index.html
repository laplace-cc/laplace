How to Write the Common App Activities List (With Examples)

The activities section is one of the most underrated parts of the Common App. Admissions officers often read it before the essays, and it's where many strong students undersell years of work in a few rushed lines.

The format

  • Up to 10 activities.
  • Position/leadership: up to 50 characters.
  • Organization name: up to 100 characters.
  • Description: up to 150 characters, including spaces.
  • Grades of participation, timing (school year, breaks), hours per week, and weeks per year.

Honors and awards go in a separate section with up to five entries.

Order matters

List your most important activities first. "Important" means where you've had the most impact, commitment, or relevance to your story, not necessarily the most impressive-sounding title. Admissions readers move fast, and your first three entries shape their impression.

Writing descriptions that show impact

With 150 characters, every word has to carry information. A simple formula: action verb + what you did + result or scale.

WeakStrong
Member of the robotics club. Helped build robots and went to competitions.Designed drivetrain for 40-lb competition robot; led 6-person build team; reached state semifinals after rebuilding auto routine.
Volunteer at the library. Helped kids with reading.Created weekly reading program for 15 elementary students; trained 4 new volunteers; program now runs year-round.

(Examples are illustrative.) Use numbers where they're honest, cut filler like "I was responsible for," and use the position field for your title so the description can focus on what you did.

Things students forget to include

  • Jobs, paid or unpaid. Colleges value work experience.
  • Family responsibilities, such as caring for siblings or relatives.
  • Independent projects: an app, a business, research, writing, a YouTube channel.
  • Summer programs and courses taken outside school.

Common mistakes

  • Exaggerating hours or titles. Your counselor and recommenders may describe the same activity.
  • Wasting space repeating the organization name in the description.
  • Listing ten shallow activities instead of a few deep ones. See our guide to building extracurriculars.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many characters is the Common App activity description?

The description is limited to 150 characters, including spaces. The position field allows 50 characters and the organization name allows 100.

Do I need to fill all 10 activity slots?

No. A few meaningful, well-described activities are better than ten thin ones. Many admitted students list fewer than ten.

Can I list a job as an activity?

Yes. Paid and unpaid work belong in the activities section, and colleges value the responsibility it shows.
