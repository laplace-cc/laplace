<h2>Step 3: Confirm the supporting documents</h2>
<ul>
<li>Recommendations requested and submitted (thank your recommenders before break).</li>
<li>Counselor materials and transcript sent.</li>
<li>Test scores sent where required or where you're choosing to submit.</li>
<li>Mid-year report: ask your counselor when it will go out.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Step 4: Financial aid</h2>
<p>File the FAFSA and any required CSS Profiles before each school's priority deadline. See <a href="/blog/css-profile-vs-fafsa/">CSS Profile vs. FAFSA</a>.</p>

<h2>Step 5: Proofread and submit early</h2>
<ul>
<li>Read every essay out loud.</li>
<li>Check that each "Why us" essay names the right school.</li>
<li>Submit at least a few days before the deadline. Portals slow down on deadline day.</li>
<li>Check each applicant portal afterward to confirm every document arrived.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Most common mistakes</h2>
<ul>
<li>Recycling a supplement with the wrong college's name in it.</li>
<li>Leaving the activities list half-finished (see our <a href="/blog/how-to-write-common-app-activities-list/">activities list guide</a>).</li>
<li>Forgetting school-specific portals and document requests after submitting.</li>
</ul>
