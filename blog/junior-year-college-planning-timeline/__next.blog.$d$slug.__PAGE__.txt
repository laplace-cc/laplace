<p>Junior year carries more weight in college admissions than any other year. It's the last full year of grades colleges see before applying, it's when most students test, and it's when the summer before senior year gets planned. Here's how to pace it.</p>

<h2>Fall</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>October: take the PSAT/NMSQT.</strong> Junior-year scores determine National Merit eligibility.</li>
<li><strong>Settle into your hardest course load.</strong> Strong junior-year grades in rigorous courses matter more than almost anything else.</li>
<li><strong>Deepen one or two activities.</strong> Junior year is the time to move from member to leader or builder. See <a href="/blog/how-to-build-extracurriculars/">building extracurriculars</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Research summer programs.</strong> Many selective programs have application deadlines between December and March. Georgia students should also watch for <a href="/blog/georgia-governors-honors-program-guide/">GHP</a> steps.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Winter</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Decide between the SAT and ACT</strong> and set a testing plan. Most students test in winter or spring of junior year so there's time for a retake. See our <a href="/blog/how-to-get-1600-sat/">SAT guide</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Submit summer program applications.</strong></li>
<li><strong>Start a working college list.</strong> Visit nearby campuses, even ones not on your list, to learn what you like. Our <a href="/blog/how-to-make-a-college-list/">college list guide</a> explains how to build one.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Spring</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Ask teachers for recommendations</strong> before the end of the year, ideally junior-year teachers in core subjects who know you well. See <a href="/blog/how-to-ask-for-letters-of-recommendation/">how to ask</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Choose senior-year courses.</strong> Keep rigor high; colleges see your senior schedule. Read <a href="/blog/how-many-ap-classes-should-i-take/">how many APs to take</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Take AP exams</strong> in May.</li>
<li><strong>Finish testing</strong> if possible, so senior fall is free for applications.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Summer before senior year</h2>
<ul>
<li>Do something meaningful: a program, research, a job, or a serious independent project.</li>
<li>Draft your <a href="/blog/how-to-write-common-app-essay/">Common App personal statement</a>.</li>
<li>Finalize your college list and decide on <a href="/blog/early-decision-vs-early-action/">Early Decision or Early Action</a>.</li>
<li>Start supplements for early schools once prompts are released (often around August 1).</li>
</ul>
